About Lena

Hi, I'm Lena. I photograph weddings, portraits, events and couples, and I care most about the moments in between: a quiet laugh, a glance, a hand held a little tighter.

I work with warm, natural light, and keep sessions relaxed, so you can be yourself instead of posing. The result is photos that still feel like you in twenty years.
